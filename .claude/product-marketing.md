## Content Rules

- Write for Indian business owners in simple English. Explain every scheme name and acronym the first time it appears.
- Facts about laws, limits and subsidy amounts must come from official sources (government portals, gazette notifications) and say "as of <month year>".
- Every post links to at least one matching service page from the table above, and ends with a call to contact us.
- Don't edit the older posts already on the site; only add new ones.
